## 🌸 About Me

Hello there! I'm **Huda**, a passionate Frontend Developer and UI Engineer based in the vibrant city of Yogyakarta, Indonesia. Currently pursuing my studies in Informatics at UNU Yogyakarta, I have a deep love for creating digital experiences that are not only highly functional but also visually stunning. 

I believe that good design is just as important as clean code. When I'm not coding, you can find me exploring new design trends or sipping on some matcha latte. 🍵✨

- 🎓 **Education:** Informatics, UNU Yogyakarta
- 💼 **Experience:** Frontend Developer @ NusaGo Digital
- 🎨 **Focus:** React.js, UI/UX Design, and Modern Web Aesthetics
